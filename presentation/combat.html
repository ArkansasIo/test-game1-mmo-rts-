07 / MILITARY RESOLUTION

Combat combines power, risk, and auditability

The frontend submits intent; the server determines whether the attack is legal, resolves the outcome, and persists every consequence.

RESOLUTION PIPELINE
01

02

03

Calculate attack, defense, race modifiers, casualties, and loot.

04

Persist

Commit resources, battle row, report, rankings, and audit event.
