06 / TIME & SETTLEMENT

The turn engine settles elapsed time transactionally

A 30-minute turn model remains responsive because settlement can be triggered by the scheduled worker or safely caught up when a commander returns.

01

Measure elapsed time

Read the world clock and player settlement timestamp. Apply a cap to prevent runaway catch-up.

02

Generate and consume

Produce resources and units, consume colony food and water, and update morale and capacity.

03

Settle queues

Complete buildings, research, shipyard work, and fleet missions whose arrival or return time has passed.

04

Record outcomes

Refresh rankings, emit notifications, write turn actions, and append audit events for review.
